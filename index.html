01 / OVERVIEW

A sense of motion.
A better way to reach.

Accurate goal reaching starts with knowing how you move.

PrO learns a compact, four-dimensional latent representation that connects proprioceptive odometry with action generation. Instead of feeding explicit displacement estimates into the policy, it learns motion features supervised by displacement and optimized for the task.

A policy pre-trained in simulation is then adapted through real-world reinforcement learning. The low-level actor uses proprioception and goal commands, without camera, LiDAR, or external odometry inputs.

02 / METHOD

Learn a motion prior.
Adapt the representation.

One compact latent space brings odometry supervision and locomotion control together.

STAGE 01 SIMULATION

Pre-train for locomotion.

PPO training in 4,096 parallel IsaacLab environments builds a locomotion prior for the Unitree Go2. Displacement supervision encourages the latent encoder to capture motion information.

ℒ = ℒPPO + λΔp ℒΔp

STAGE 02 REAL WORLD

Refine through experience.

TD3 post-training adapts the latent encoder to real-world feedback. The policy head stays frozen to preserve the learned action distribution while the representation becomes more task-aligned.
